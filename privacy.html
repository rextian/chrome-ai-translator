Overview The Extension is an AI text translation tool. It provides translation, definition and text polishing for text you manually select on web pages, only when you actively trigger the function. This policy describes how we collect, use, store and share your data, and your rights. The Extension will NOT automatically read webpage content, silently collect browsing history, track users for advertising, or collect irrelevant personal data. ## 2. Data We Access & When We only access the text you select or submit for translation, after your explicit action (select text, click right‑click translate menu, input text in popup). - ✅ Collected: text you manually select or submit for translation - ❌ NOT automatically read: full page content, passwords, form data, cookies, browsing history - ❌ NOT collected: location, contact list, persistent device identifiers Submitted text is only used for the current translation request. We do not store your original text or translation results on our developer server after translation. ## 3. How We Use Your Data 1. Provide translation service: Your submitted text is sent over HTTPS to the AI translation provider you choose, to fetch and display translation results. 2. Store preferences locally: Browser `storage` is used to save your settings such as target language, AI model, popup style and hotkeys. These settings stay locally in your browser and are NOT uploaded to our server. 3. Core feature support: Host permissions, contextMenus and offscreen documents are used for translation popups, text preprocessing and copy-to-clipboard functions, for translation purposes only. We will NOT use your translated text for AI model training, marketing or analytics. ## 4. Data Sharing Your translation text is forwarded only to the AI service provider you configure, governed by that provider’s own privacy policy. We do not sell or share your translation content with unrelated third parties. We may disclose data only when required by applicable law or valid legal process. ## 5. Data Retention - Your preferences are saved locally in browser storage. You may reset or clear them in extension settings anytime. - Translation text is processed temporarily in memory and discarded immediately after the request completes. We do not persist translation records on our backend. - We do not run a dedicated database to store your translation history. ## 6. Permission Justification - Host permissions: Read selected text and inject lightweight scripts for translation popup, only on user trigger. - contextMenus: Add “Translate selected text” entry to browser right‑click menu. - offscreen: Handle network requests, text processing and clipboard logic unsupported in service workers. - storage: Save local translation preferences. - Remote code: Load necessary scripts for API request and rendering only on user activation. No untrusted arbitrary code. ## 7. Your Rights - You may stop using or uninstall the Extension at any time; local stored preferences will be cleared after uninstallation. - You can choose your own AI translation provider and control where your translation data is sent. - You may contact us via the verified developer email for privacy inquiries. ## 8. Children The Extension is intended for general users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. ## 9. Policy Changes We may revise this Privacy Policy to comply with platform rules or product updates. Continued use of the Extension after changes means you accept the revised policy. ## 10.
